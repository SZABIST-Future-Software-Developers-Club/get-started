About SFSDC

SZABIST Future Software Developers Club (SFSDC) is the premier technology club at SZABIST University, operating under the Student Societies Council (SSC). We are a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering technical excellence and innovation.

Our Vision

To build SZABIST's largest community of developers, innovators, and tech leaders.

Our Mission & Objectives

To bridge the gap between academia and industry by empowering students with practical skills.

Our Leadership

Meet the dedicated team driving the vision of SFSDC forward.

Executive Committee (EC)

The strategic leadership team responsible for governance, university liaison, and final decision-making.

President/Chairperson

Overlooks all club operations, aligns strategies with the SZABIST Student Council (SSC), and serves as the primary representative.

General Secretary

Manages internal operations, coordinates between different club departments, manages documentation, official correspondence, meeting minutes, and club records.

Treasurer / Finance Director

Manages the club's budget, funding requests through the SSC, and financial tracking for events.

Core Committee (Directors, Heads & Leads)

The execution team responsible for leading specific departments and organizing technical initiatives.

Director - Software Development

To lead all technical projects, workshops and developer teams.

Director - UI/UX

To lead design language, posters, and product design sessions.

Director - Competitive Programming & Hackathons

To organize coding contests, hackathons and training sessions.

Director - Marketing & PR

To manage social media, content creation, and university outreach.

Technical Lead

Oversees coding bootcamps, hackathons, and curriculum design for BSCS/BSSE students.

Operations & Logistics Lead

Handles venue bookings, equipment setup, and event flow management at SZABIST.

Marketing & PR Lead

Manages the club's campus presence, announcements, and outreach to the student body.

Graphics & Media Lead

Responsible for designing visual assets, posters, social media banners, and event photography.

General Body Subcommittee (Officers / Associates)

The entry-level execution workforce. These positions are perfect for junior students looking to gain experience.

Technical Officers

Assist in conducting coding workshops, mentoring peers, and evaluating coding challenges.

Operations & PR Officers

Help with on-ground event management, registrations, and classroom promotions.

Content Writers & Designers

Draft captions, write newsletters, and create digital graphics.
