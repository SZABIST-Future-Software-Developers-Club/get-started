Cookies & browser storage

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Site preferences

The theme and language selectors save your choices in your browser's local storage under the keys sfsdc-theme and sfsdc-language. This storage is used to remember your preferences on future visits; it is not a cookie and is not sent to SFSDC as part of a page request. You can clear it using your browser's site-data controls.

Third-party cookies

Language options use translations bundled with this website and do not use a translation service. Google Fonts, the Tailwind CDN, Font Awesome's CDN, and external links may connect your browser to third-party services. Those providers may set or read their own cookies under their policies.

Managing cookies

You can use your browser settings to block or remove cookies and local storage. Blocking third-party services may affect translation or externally hosted page resources. For details about information and third-party services, read our Privacy Policy.
