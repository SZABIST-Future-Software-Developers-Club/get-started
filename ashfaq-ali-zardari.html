Background & education

Ashfaq Ali Zardari is a software engineer, developer, and founder based in Karachi. He is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at SZABIST University and has prior software engineering diplomas from Aptech Learning.

His professional focus includes .NET technologies, Web APIs, and SQL. In 2023, he founded Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store LLP, a software development and digital goods company in Karachi.

SFSDC leadership

As Founder and President/Chairperson, Ashfaq leads the club's operations, represents SFSDC, and aligns its strategy with the SZABIST Student Council (SSC). The club's structure brings together executive leadership, directors and leads, and a general body subcommittee.

SFSDC focuses on creating opportunities for students to learn, collaborate, and develop practical skills through technical initiatives and community activities.
