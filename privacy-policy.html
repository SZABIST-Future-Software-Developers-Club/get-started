Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Information you provide

Browsing this informational website does not require an account. If you contact SFSDC by email or WhatsApp, the information you choose to send (such as your name, contact details, and message) is received by the relevant communication provider and club representatives so they can respond.

Information collected by this website

This static website does not provide a user account, contact form, or first-party analytics service. Your browser and the services used to deliver page resources may process technical information such as an IP address, device details, and request logs as part of providing those services.

Third-party services

The site links to services including Google Play, Google Fonts, Font Awesome's CDN, social networks, Google Forms, and WhatsApp. Following a link or loading a third-party service may allow that provider to collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. SFSDC does not control those services or their data practices. Language options use translations bundled with this website and do not send page text to a translation service.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to use third-party links or the translation feature. Theme and language preferences are stored in your browser's local storage; see our Cookies page for details. For privacy questions, contact support.sfsdc@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
