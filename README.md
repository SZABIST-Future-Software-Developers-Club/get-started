# get-started
SZABIST Future Software Developers CLUB (SFSDC) is the premier technology club at SZABIST University, operating under the Student Societies Council (SSC).

## Website pages

- `index.html` — club information and contact details.
- `teams.html` — active member profiles and the Executive, Core, and General Body hierarchy, including vacancies marked as hiring in process.
- `ashfaq-ali-zardari.html` and `laiba-aamir.html` — separate member profiles with ATS CV and professional-profile PDF downloads.
- `news.html` — static news and updates with paginated cards, leadership updates, and the Team announcement highlight.
- `blog.html` — static blog articles with paginated cards for SFSDC leadership and student development.
- `app.html` — official SFSDC Google Play download page (package ID: `co.ashfaqalizardaristore.sfsdc.ssc.szabist`).
- `privacy-policy.html`, `terms-and-conditions.html`, and `cookies.html` — website policies.
- `sitemap.html` — links to the website pages and sections.

The shared controls in `assets/site.js` provide System, Light, and Dark themes and English plus Urdu, Sindhi, Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Japanese, Turkish, Persian, Korean, Vietnamese, and Thai. Translated interface labels and page headings are bundled with the site; no translation service or network request is used. Theme and language choices are remembered in browser local storage. Shared styles are in `assets/site.css`.

The downloadable Laiba Aamir CV and professional profile were prepared from her supplied resume and include her contact details and student identifier as approved for publication.
