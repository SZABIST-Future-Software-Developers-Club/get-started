Professional summary

Laiba Aamir is a Computer Science student at SZABIST University and a product and UI/UX designer with practical experience in digital product development. She works on intuitive web and mobile experiences, interactive prototypes, design systems, and visual assets, using user-centered thinking and AI-assisted workflows to support ideation and design iteration.

SFSDC leadership

As Director of UI/UX in the SFSDC Core Committee, Laiba helps lead the club's design vertical. Her responsibilities include managing UI/UX bootcamps, critiquing software designs, and helping collaborative projects combine aesthetic quality with usable, functional experiences.

Her stated focus is creative vision and leadership for UI/UX design. The committee role is part of SFSDC, SZABIST Student Council (SSC), SZABIST University.
